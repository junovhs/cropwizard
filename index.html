Nothing is uploaded. Images stay in your browser.

Batch crop

Every image gets its own crop, and you make each one.

  1. Drop a stack, or choose them below.
  2. Drag and scroll the image until the frame holds what you want.
  3. Press ↵, or hit Finalize this crop, to keep it and jump to the next one.
  4. At the end, export the lot as a single ZIP.

Changed your mind? Turn Batch off in the sidebar.
